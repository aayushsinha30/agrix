# **App Name**: AGRIX

## Core Features:

- Farmer Profiling: Collect and store farmer's profile (Name, Location, Farm Size, Soil Type, Main Crops) along with language preferences.
- Smart Crop Suggestion: Suggest the best crops for the season with consideration of expected profit margins. This acts as a decision support tool.
- Market Price Display: Display real-time market prices of crops.
- Disease Detection: Detect crop diseases based on uploaded photos and suggest treatment options. This acts as a decision support tool.
- Weather Alerts: Provide weather forecasts and alerts relevant to farming.

## Style Guidelines:

- Use a vibrant, natural color palette with a base of earthy greens and sky blues, complemented by warm tones of golden yellow and sunset orange. Like the reference image, different shades of brown and green must be implemented.
- Incorporate desaturated pastel shades to create a calming and inviting atmosphere, reminiscent of a serene countryside.
- Use a friendly and approachable font such as 'Poppins' for headings and 'Open Sans' for body text. Adjust sizes for readability on various devices.
- Design custom icons that illustrate agricultural activities and concepts with a playful and illustrative style, similar to those shown in the reference image. Consider adding icons for different languages.
- Employ a layered background with illustrations of farmland, crops, and natural elements to bring depth and visual interest to the layout. Similar to the reference image.
- Divide the screen into clear, digestible sections with rounded containers and subtle shadows to provide a modern touch, similar to the reference image.
- Add subtle animations and transitions to guide users through the app, enhance interactivity, and provide feedback, making it feel alive and responsive.
